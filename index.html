Quick start

A short guide to inspecting and recording a weekly prediction.

  1. Move around

    Click or tap a ligand to zoom in; click white space to zoom out. Drag to rotate, right-drag or Ctrl-drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom.

  2. Choose a layout

    Show all overlays poses, One at a time isolates them, and Grid compares them side by side.

  3. Review the evidence

    Select your best pose and Reject any you rule out. Surface and H-bonds are optional viewing aids.

  4. Save and revisit

    Record vote saves each question immediately, even if you do not finish. Update vote records a revision without erasing your earlier submission; the arrows let you skip or revisit questions.
